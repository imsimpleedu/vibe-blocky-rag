# COMPONENT: OLED Text Display Screen (OLED 顯示器)
[DEVICE_CODE] "4"
[PIN_ID] "0" to "4"
[STRICT_FORMAT] ["OUTPUT", "4", "<pin>", "<mode_0_1_2>", <content_payload>, "N/A", "N/A"]
[PARAMETERS]
* mode_0_1_2: "0" (Clear Screen), "1" (No Newline), "2" (New Line)
* content_payload: Can be a static String (e.g., "Hello!") or a Variable Store Block

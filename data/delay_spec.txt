# COMPONENT: Built-in System Delay (等待延時)
[DEVICE_CODE] "0"
[PIN_ID] "0"
[STRICT_FORMAT] ["OUTPUT", "0", "0", "DELAY", <number_seconds>, null]
[EXPLANATION] Suspends execution of the thread. Supports floating numbers (e.g., 0.5).
[LOGIC RATIONALE] Use this to prevent hardware from flapping or to create blinking effects.
